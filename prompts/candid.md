# Phone Snapshot

Use this mode for the most accidental-feeling image. Start with one clear action, then allow only one or two capture flaws that match the chosen era:

- framing shifted slightly to one side
- focus catching the background for a moment
- a small amount of motion blur while she turns
- bright sky or shop light clipping gently
- mixed indoor light affecting white balance

Keep the subject recognizable and the scene readable. A candid snapshot is not a poorly made image; it is a moment worth keeping despite technical flaws.

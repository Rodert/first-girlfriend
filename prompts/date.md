# First Date

Choose a low-stakes, recognizable place: a milk-tea counter, cinema entrance, bookstore, casual restaurant, or a station meeting point.

The feeling is not romance-poster perfection. Include a little uncertainty: a hand around a paper cup, checking a ticket, deciding where to stand, or a small laugh just after an awkward pause. Keep styling practical and location-appropriate.

For `first-date`, frame the camera at conversational distance. For `years-later`, let a detail such as a paper ticket or old shopping-mall light carry the memory rather than making the subject perform nostalgia.

# Years Later

Use this mode to make the photograph feel rediscovered, not artificially aged. Choose a specific past era and let the capture technology do most of the work. Add at most one supporting memory cue: a faded shopping-mall interior, an old train ticket, a discontinued-looking drink cup, or a familiar neighborhood before redevelopment.

The adult subject does not need to look sad. Emotional distance comes from the photograph's context: an unguarded glance, unused space in the frame, a transit window, or the knowledge that the moment is over. Avoid fake film borders, timestamp overlays, melodramatic sepia, and captions embedded in the image.

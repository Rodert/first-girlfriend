# Winter

Choose an intimate but ordinary source of warmth: a convenience-store window, a bus shelter, a takeaway cup, a station platform, or a street after dusk.

Use practical outerwear, a scarf, and restrained gestures such as warming hands on a cup or tucking hair into a collar. Condensed breath can be a subtle cue in cold air, but do not make it theatrical. Let mixed shop light or a slightly cool white balance support the memory.

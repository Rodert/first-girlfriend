# Summer

Favor late afternoon or early evening over a cinematic golden-hour spectacle. Useful locations include a shaded sidewalk, bus stop, bicycle rack, corner shop, riverside, or a quiet residential street.

Use breathable everyday clothes: a white T-shirt, light shirt, denim, canvas shoes, or a simple tote. Add one physical trace of heat or movement, such as hair lifted by a breeze, softened shadows, a cold drink, or a bicycle just coming to a stop.

The image should feel observed in passing, with natural color and room for mundane background detail.

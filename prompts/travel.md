# Travel

Make travel feel incidental rather than aspirational. Good settings include a train window seat, ferry deck, side street in an old neighborhood, hostel breakfast table, or a sea wall after a long walk.

Include a modest travel detail: a folded ticket, backpack strap, wind-tossed hair, unpacked snack, or reflection in a train window. Avoid destination-postcard poses and keep the subject's expression occupied by the moment rather than the camera.

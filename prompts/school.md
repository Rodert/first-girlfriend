# After School And Library

Use these cues for adult campus life, work-study routines, or graduation-era memories. Never describe a minor or use school-uniform fetish framing.

## Locations

- University library window seat with borrowed books and a canvas bag
- Evening bus stop after a campus class or office shift
- Campus walkway after graduation rehearsal
- Quiet cafe near a university, laptop and notes still open

## Mood

Keep the gesture small: looking up from a page, shifting a bag on one shoulder, or turning back after hearing her name. For `crush`, use a respectful distance and a brief, uncertain glance. For `girlfriend`, let the acknowledgment feel familiar.

Natural window light, ordinary button-down shirts, knit cardigans, worn sneakers, scribbled notes, a light breeze, and slightly untidy hair work well. Do not turn the space into an immaculate editorial set.
